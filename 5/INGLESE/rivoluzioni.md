
## **1st Industrial Revolution (mid-1700s to early 1800s)**

🔹 **Steam engine (improved)** – _James Watt_ improved the steam engine and patented his design in **1769**, transforming machine power and industry.  
🔹 **Spinning Jenny (textile machine)** – _James Hargreaves_ invented it around **1764–1765** to speed up yarn production.  
🔹 **Water frame** – _Richard Arkwright_ adapted a spinning machine to use water power and helped create modern factories (late **1760s–1770s**).  
🔹 **Spinning mule** – _Samuel Crompton_ created this in **1779**, improving thread quality and productivity.  
🔹 **Power loom** – _Edmund Cartwright_ patented the power loom in **1785** for weaving cloth faster.  
🔹 **Steam locomotive (railway engine)** – _Richard Trevithick_ built the first steam locomotive in **1803** and _George Stephenson_’s “Rocket” ran in **1830**.  
🔹 **Cotton gin** – _Eli Whitney_ patented it in **1793** to separate cotton seeds quickly.

**Main idea:** Mechanisation of production with steam power and early factories.

---

## **2nd Industrial Revolution (mid-1800s to early 1900s)**

🔹 **Electric incandescent light** – _Joseph Swan_ and _Thomas Edison_ independently developed practical electric light bulbs by **1878–1880**.  
🔹 **Telephone** – _Alexander Graham Bell_ patented the telephone in **1876**.  
🔹 **Air brake for trains** – _George Westinghouse_ patented it in **1872** to make rail travel safer.  
🔹 **Internal-combustion engine developments** – _Nikolaus Otto_ developed the four-stroke engine in **1876**; _Gottlieb Daimler_ and _Karl Benz_ developed gasoline engines and early automobiles in the **1880s**.  
🔹 **Steel production process (Bessemer)** – _Henry Bessemer_ introduced the steel-making process in **1856**, revolutionizing construction and machinery.  
🔹 **Assembly line (mass production)** – _Henry Ford_ perfected assembly-line car production in **1913**, massively increasing output.

**Main idea:** Electricity, steel, mass production, and communication revolutionised industry and everyday life.

---

## **3rd Industrial Revolution (mid-20th century onward)**

🔹 **Transistor** – _John Bardeen, Walter Brattain, William Shockley_ developed the first working transistor in **1947**, launching modern electronics.  
🔹 **Integrated circuit** – _Jack Kilby_ and _Robert Noyce_ created the integrated circuit in **1958–1959**, allowing multiple electronic components on one chip.  
🔹 **Microprocessor** – _Intel_ released the first commercially available microprocessor, the **Intel 4004**, in **1971**.  
🔹 **Personal computers and Internet foundations** – development continued through the **1970s–1990s** with mainframes, PCs, and networking, evolving from ARPANET to the early Internet.

**Main idea:** Digital electronics, computers, and networking transformed production, communication, and society.

---

## **4th Industrial Revolution / Industry 4.0 (21st century – today)**

Unlike older revolutions, the fourth is **not defined by single inventions**, but by a combination of advanced digital technologies:

🔹 **Internet of Things (IoT)** – networks of connected devices and sensors exchanging data in real time.  
🔹 **Cyber-physical systems and smart factories** – integration of machines, software, and automation.  
🔹 **3D printing (additive manufacturing)** and **robotics** – flexible and customised production.  
🔹 **Artificial Intelligence (AI), Big Data, cloud computing** – advanced data analysis and decision-making.

🕐 **Beginning:** The term “Industry 4.0” was first used in **2011** in Germany.

**Main idea:** Integration of digital, physical, and biological systems with smart automation.
