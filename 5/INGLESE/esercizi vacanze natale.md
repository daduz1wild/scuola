### **1. True / False**

1. _Industry 4.0 and the 4th Industrial Revolution are synonyms._  
    **True.** Industry 4.0 is the name given to the Fourth Industrial Revolution.
    
2. _Industry 4.0 focuses on automated production which is monitored with sensor-based technologies._  
    **True.** It emphasises smart, sensor-monitored automation.
    
3. _3D printing is a design process._  
    **False.** 3D printing is a manufacturing process based on a computer design, not the design itself.
    
4. _A drone does not have a pilot._  
    **True.** Delivery drones are unmanned aerial vehicles without an onboard pilot.
    
5. _Drones are used to transport heavy goods._  
    **False.** Delivery drones normally carry lightweight packages.
    
6. _IoT only refers to industrial applications._  
    **False.** IoT also includes everyday smart devices like household objects.

### **3. Internet / History**

1. **Who invented the steam engine in 1784?**  
    James Watt improved the existing steam engine, making it much more efficient and practical. His work in the late 1700s was fundamental for industrial use.
    
2. **When did Thomas Edison invent the first long-lasting electric bulb?**  
    Thomas Alva Edison developed a commercially practical long-lasting incandescent light bulb around **1879**.
    
3. **The 2nd Industrial Revolution is also associated with mass production. Quote an early example of mass production.**  
    An early example of mass production is the use of **assembly lines**, such as those later developed by Henry Ford, which allowed standardized large-scale manufacturing.
    
4. **Think of computer networks. What happened in 1969?**  
    In 1969 the first ARPANET connection was made, allowing two computers to communicate over a packet-switched network.
    
5. **When was the Internet invented?**  
    The Internet is considered to have been born when ARPANET adopted the TCP/IP protocol on **January 1, 1983**, allowing global network interconnection.
    
6. **Although the official birth of the 4th Industrial Revolution is 2011, when does the expression ‘Internet of Things’ (IoT) date back to?**  
    The term “Internet of Things” was first coined in **1999** by Kevin Ashton.

### **What is laser eye surgery used for?**

Laser eye surgery is mainly used to **correct vision problems** such as short-sightedness (myopia), long-sightedness (hyperopia), and astigmatism by reshaping the cornea.

### **Can children be subject to laser eye surgery?**

No. Laser eye surgery is usually **not suitable for children or young people** because their vision may still be changing.

### **Laser eye surgery specific questions**

1. **How many types of laser eye surgery are there? What are they?**  
    There are several types, including **LASIK, LASEK, PRK**, and SMILE. All reshape the cornea to improve vision.
    
2. **Is laser eye surgery always successful after the first treatment?**  
    No. Some people need additional surgery to achieve the best possible result.
    
3. **When might people suffer from visual disturbances?**  
    Visual disturbances such as glare or halos at night may occur after surgery but usually improve over time.
    
4. **How long can red marks on the white of the eye last?**  
    Red marks can appear after surgery and usually fade within about **one month**.
