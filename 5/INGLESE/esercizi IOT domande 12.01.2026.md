## **Section A: History & Definitions**

1. **What 1969 network development served as the precursor to the modern internet and IoT?**
   **ARPANET**, developed in 1969, which connected computers across different locations.

2. **Which university hosted the first "smart" Coca-Cola machine in 1982?**
   **Carnegie Mellon University**.

3. **Who coined the term "Internet of Things" in 1999?**
   **Kevin Ashton**, while working at Procter and Gamble.

4. **Define Edge Computing and explain why it is important for IoT responsiveness.**
   **Edge Computing** is the processing of data close to the source (on local devices rather than in the cloud). It is important because it reduces latency and makes IoT devices faster and more responsive.

5. **What technology allows digital tracking of physical items via radio waves?**
   **RFID (Radio-Frequency Identification)**.

## **Section B: Applications & Impact**

1. **How did the first iPhone (2007) impact the growth of the IoT ecosystem?**
   It accelerated IoT adoption by popularizing smartphones, which allow remote control of smart devices through apps.

2. **Give an example of how Tesla uses IoT in its vehicles.**
   **Tesla Autopilot**, which uses IoT sensors, cameras, and radar to assist driving.

3. **In what ways did IoT assist healthcare professionals during the COVID-19 pandemic?**
   Through connected thermal cameras, contact tracing devices, and health-monitoring wearables that provided real-time data.

4. **Explain the role of IoT in Aquaculture based on the 2018 Toyota/Microsoft partnership.**
   IoT was used for smart water management, using AI to count fish, assess quantities, and optimize water flow in fish farming.

5. **How does IoT support "Smart Cities" in terms of infrastructure?**
   By enabling traffic control, waste management, and energy conservation through connected systems.

6. **List two ways IoT provides "Assistive Technology" for the elderly or disabled.**

* Voice control for people with vision or mobility impairments
* Safety sensors that detect falls or medical emergencies

## **Section C: Future & Risks**

1. **What are the two primary technical enablers that will drive future IoT growth?**
   **5G** and **Edge Computing**.

2. **According to the text, what is the "Governance Gap"?**
   It is the gap between potential IoT risks and society’s ability to address them through legislation, industry standards, and self-governance.

3. **Identify three major risks associated with the widespread adoption of IoT.**

* Cybersecurity threats
* Privacy violations
* Surveillance and control of movement or expression

4. **By 2025, how many connected devices does the World Economic Forum predict will exist?**
   **41.6 billion connected devices**.


The First Industrial Revolution began in the late **18th** (1) century and was characterised by the use of the **steam** (2) engine. Production moved from small workshops to **factories** (3), especially in the textile industry. Coal and **steam power** (4) became the main sources of energy, and many people moved from the countryside to rapidly growing **cities** (5).

The Second Industrial Revolution started around **1870** (6) and introduced new energy sources such as **electricity** (7) and oil. Industries adopted **mass** (8) production methods, allowing goods to be produced more quickly and cheaply. Important inventions of this period included the **telephone** (9), the light bulb and the internal combustion engine.

The Third Industrial Revolution, also known as the **Digital** (10) Revolution, began in the second half of the 20th century. It was marked by the spread of **computers** (11), electronics and the Internet. Factories increasingly used **automation** (12) and robots, leading to higher productivity and a growth in the **service** (13) sector.

The Fourth Industrial Revolution is still ongoing and is based on advanced digital technologies. It involves the use of artificial **intelligence** (14), big data and the Internet of **Things** (15). This revolution is changing the way people work, communicate and interact with both machines and **systems** (16).
